About Dopamine Rehab

Dopamine Rehab is a browser extension designed to help you regain control over your digital habits.

By allowing you to configure specific actions (like grayscale or closing tabs) for addictive websites during certain hours, it helps you stay focused and reduce mindless scrolling.

How it works

  • Grayscale: Makes the website less visually stimulating.
  • Force Close: Automatically closes the tab to prevent access.
  • Remove HTML Selectors: Hides specific distracting elements (like infinite scroll feeds or notification badges).

Privacy

All your settings are stored locally in your browser and synced using your Chrome account. We do not collect or transmit any of your browsing data.

Backup

You can download your current settings as a JSON file for backup or to move them to another browser.
